# Digital Garden

## About

Digital Garden is a personal repository that documents my journey throughout university and beyond. It brings together the projects I have worked on as an Information Systems student with a focus on AI and Data Science, alongside achievements and awards earned outside the academic environment.

This space is meant to reflect growth. Some projects are polished and complete, while others capture experimentation, learning, and progress over time. Together, they form a portfolio that represents both my technical development and my broader professional journey.

## What This Repository Includes

* University course projects and technical assignments
* Independent and exploratory projects
* Hackathons, competitions, and extracurricular initiatives
* Awards, recognitions, and notable achievements
* Ongoing work that reflects continuous learning

## Purpose

The goal of this repository is to serve as a long-term record of my work and a foundation for my professional portfolio. It highlights how my skills and interests evolve through hands-on experience, problem solving, and real-world applications.

## Notes

This repository is continuously updated. Content may change as projects are refined, new work is added, and goals evolve over time.
